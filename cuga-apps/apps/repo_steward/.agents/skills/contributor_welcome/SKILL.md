# Contributor Welcome

Draft a short, warm comment to welcome a first-time contributor. The goal: make them feel invited, point them to the right resources, and set realistic expectations without promising anything.

## Output format

A markdown comment ready to paste into GitHub. Two or three short paragraphs.

Structure:
1. Genuine thanks for the contribution.
2. One pointer: CONTRIBUTING.md, code-of-conduct, or a relevant doc.
3. What happens next (in general terms — no timelines).

## Rules

- Do NOT use exclamation marks — warm, not performative.
- Never promise a review date, merge, or that someone specific will respond.
- Do not apologize for the project's pace.
- Close with a neutral sign-off, not the maintainer's name (the agent doesn't know who they are).
- Keep it under 120 words.
